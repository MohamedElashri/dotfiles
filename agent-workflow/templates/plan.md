# Implementation plan

Use one ledger for the task. Preserve established tracking conventions; temporary
personal notes may stay untracked without changing the project's ignore rules.

## Outcome and scope

State the requested behavior, acceptance criteria, constraints, and exclusions.

## Observed starting point

Record relevant entry points, subsystems, existing commands, user changes, and
baseline failures. Distinguish observations from assumptions and unknowns.

## Steps

For each meaningful step, record deliverables, acceptance criteria, status
(pending, in progress, complete, blocked, or deferred), concise completion
evidence, and any decision or deviation affecting later work. Keep all steps
here instead of creating separate phase plans or reports.

## Verification

Identify focused checks, integration/smoke checks, and broader checks appropriate
to risk. Record actual outcomes, unavailable checks, and material remaining risks.
